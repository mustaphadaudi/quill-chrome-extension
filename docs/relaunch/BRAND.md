# Dictate — brand brief v0.1

Status: product name and high-level direction approved. This brief records direction, not final artwork, copy or interface approval. See [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) for approval history and [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for interface behaviour.

## Approved direction

**Dictate** is a premium writing assistant with a fictional authoritarian mascot obsessed with good writing. The character treats grammar and clarity as matters of absurd national importance. Satire should expose the character's pompous authority; it should not belittle the person writing.

The brand should feel at home in a satirical open-world-game universe: exaggerated confidence, fictional institutions, mock propaganda and ridiculous bureaucratic seriousness. Rockstar/GTA is a tonal reference, not a visual template. Create original logos, characters, typography, interface compositions and artwork; do not copy protected branding or imply affiliation. The mascot must be fictional rather than a portrait of a real dictator.

The approved palette direction is **cream / orange / black**. Exact colour values, proportions and accessible light/dark combinations remain to be designed.

The core product stays clean, premium and easy to use. Humour belongs in surrounding microcopy and the brand universe, with stronger personality in onboarding illustrations and promotional concepts than in inline editing controls. A themed message must never imply that text was checked, changed or transmitted when it was not.

## Voice and placement

Use concise, dry, overconfident humour in low-stakes brand moments. Keep correction explanations useful and neutral. Consent, privacy, permissions, errors and recovery instructions must be plain and truthful. The user's choice to accept, dismiss or disable remains clear.

Illustrative copy only, pending approval:

- Onboarding brand line: “Welcome to the Ministry of Clarity.”
- Completion accent, only after the user accepts corrections: “Order restored.”
- Functional controls: “Accept”, “Dismiss”, “Undo”.

“Ministry of Clarity” is an example of the fictional universe, not an approved institution name or tagline. Do not treat exploratory copy or previous concept boards as final assets.
