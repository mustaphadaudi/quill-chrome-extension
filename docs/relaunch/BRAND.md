# Dictate — brand brief v0.2

Status: product name and A+D hybrid visual direction approved. Visual mockups are approved as direction, not pixel-perfect UI specifications or final production vector assets. Final copy, interface behaviour and production exports still require approval. See [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) for approval history and [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for interface behaviour.

## Approved direction

**Dictate** is a premium writing assistant with a fictional authoritarian mascot obsessed with good writing. The character treats grammar and clarity as matters of absurd national importance. Satire should expose the character's pompous authority; it should not belittle the person writing.

The brand should feel at home in a satirical open-world-game universe: exaggerated confidence, fictional institutions, mock propaganda and ridiculous bureaucratic seriousness. Rockstar/GTA is a tonal reference, not a visual template. Create original logos, characters, typography, interface compositions and artwork; do not copy protected branding or imply affiliation. The mascot must be fictional rather than a portrait of a real dictator.

### Selected A+D hybrid

- **A — Supreme Editor:** an exaggerated, original fictional general with a moustache, sunglasses, ceremonial cap and writing-themed decorations. Use this character primarily in marketing and onboarding. Detailed poses, expressions and costume artwork remain to be refined; do not use real authoritarian insignia.
- **D — State Emblem:** a minimalist crowned fountain-pen nib in orange/black is the selected logo and extension-icon direction, paired with a strong **Dictate** wordmark. Develop small-size and monochrome variants from this emblem; the full character is not the toolbar icon.
- **Brand world:** the satirical fictional **Ministry of Clarity**, with witty propaganda-inspired copy. This approves the fictional setting, not every proposed tagline or message.

### Palette and typography status

The approved palette direction is **warm cream, vivid orange, charcoal and sand**, with limited **sunset orange and sage** accents. Orange/black remains the emblem treatment. Exact proportions, semantic status colours and accessible light/dark combinations require verification.

Any hex values shown in the approved reference are **preliminary, not implementation-locked until verified**. The source image is not accessible in this checkout, and the retrieved conversation does not expose those values; do not invent or sample substitute colours. Transcribe the shown hex values after source transfer, retain their preliminary status, then verify usage and contrast before approving implementation tokens.

**Bebas Neue for headings and Inter for body text are proposed**, not final typography approval. Confirm weights, fallbacks, licensing and readability in the UX review.

The core product stays clean, premium and easy to use. Humour belongs in surrounding microcopy and the brand universe, with stronger personality in onboarding illustrations and promotional concepts than in inline editing controls. A themed message must never imply that text was checked, changed or transmitted when it was not.

## Voice and placement

Use concise, dry, overconfident humour in low-stakes brand moments. Keep correction explanations useful and neutral. Consent, privacy, permissions, errors and recovery instructions must be plain and truthful. The user's choice to accept, dismiss or disable remains clear.

Illustrative copy only, pending approval:

- Onboarding brand line: “Welcome to the Ministry of Clarity.”
- Completion accent, only after the user accepts corrections: “Order restored.”
- Functional controls: “Accept”, “Dismiss”, “Undo”.

“Ministry of Clarity” is the approved fictional setting. The example sentences above remain illustrative copy; visual concept approval does not make them final product messages or production assets.
